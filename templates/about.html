About Stock Predictor

Welcome to Stock Predictor! This website is the culmination of my final year project, where I explored the fascinating intersection of finance and artificial intelligence. Through this journey, I delved into the world of deep learning to unlock insights hidden within historical market data.

By analyzing multiple advanced models and rigorously comparing their performance, I was able to select the best approach for predicting stock prices. While the predictions generated by the AI are not 100% accurate—reflecting the inherent uncertainties of the stock market—they do come remarkably close, offering valuable guidance and an interesting glimpse into potential market trends.

My goal with Stock Predictor was not only to create a technically robust system but also to design a user-friendly interface that makes complex data accessible and engaging. Whether you're an experienced investor or simply curious about how modern technology can shed light on financial markets, I hope you find this tool both informative and enjoyable.

Thank you for visiting and taking the time to explore what I've built. I genuinely hope you enjoy using Stock Predictor as much as I enjoyed creating it!
